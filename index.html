🚚 PROJECT 02

Hyper-local P2P Delivery

🗓️ Aug 2024 – Nov 2024  ·  🛠️ PM & Python Dev  ·  👥 Team: 2 Members

Campus Gig Economy converting movement data into logistics networks

⚠️ 01. Problem Discovery

"Potential Demand 'Explodes',
Last-mile trapped in 'Inefficiency'"

📉 Inefficiency

Traditional riders move to 'catch orders'. But campuses have complex terrain (hills, security) and restricted entry, making last-mile costs abnormally high.

😩 Unmet Demand

Students want coffee but give up due to short break times (10~15 min) preventing round trips to cafes.

💡 Insight

"No need for professional riders. Students moving to that building anyway can deliver."

🗺️ 02. Solution Strategy

"Route-based Dispatch, not Location-based"

🔄 Paradigm Shift

  • (As-Is) Traditional: Rider moves to order origin → Pickup → Delivery (Move to Deliver)
  • (To-Be) Proposed: Orderer picks last-mile (dest), matches student moving there (Deliver while Moving)

⚙️ Mechanism

Student couriers pick up coffee "on their way to class"
This drastically reduces Delivery Cost and maximizes mover efficiency.

📊 03. Market Validation

"Beyond technical possibility, verifying Willingness to Participate"

🙋🏻‍♂️

User Survey

Surveyed 100 students. 68% positive response: "Will deliver if route matches 90%".
Lowered psychological barrier by framing as 'Monetizing Idle Time'.

💰

Unit Economics

Matched with fee under 1,000 KRW (70% cheaper than standard 3,000 KRW).
Validated potential for Micro-delivery market.

💓 04. Algorithm Comparison
🕰️ Legacy
Rider moves separately for pickup
(High Cost / Inefficient)
⚡ Campus (Proposed)
Pickup on the way to destination
(Low Cost / Optimized)
💻 05. Tech & Verification

"VRP Simulation using Google OR-Tools"

🧬 Algorithm

Derived optimal routes using Google OR-Tools library to match multiple students (Vehicles) with orders (Nodes).

⚠️ Constraints

Set realistic variables as constraints, not just distance.

  • Time Window (Class time)
  • Vertical Time (Building entry)
  • Walking Speed

✅ Simulation Result

Modeled campus map data and simulated with Python.

40%
🏆 06. Outcome & Impact
📉

Cost Efficiency

Converted 40% distance reduction to cost.
Delivery cost 3,000 KRW → 900 KRW.
70% Saving Effect proven.

📦

Deliverable

Developed Google OR-Tools Optimal Route Matching algorithm prototype based on actual campus map.

💡 07. Retrospective (KPT)

"Discovered realistic operation issues (Supply-Demand Sync) and pondered improvements."

Keep

Designed a model utilizing idle resources without extra labor input by approaching with Route-based instead of Location-based.

Problem

Peak Time Issue: Discovered 'Supply-Demand Sync' phenomenon where orders and couriers surge during breaks and vanish during classes. Supply Crunch occurred as couriers also had to attend class.

Try

Dynamic Pricing: Induce supply by paying 1.5x incentives during specific times (during class, free periods).
Pickup Zone: Optimize operations by installing 'Pickup Zones' near classrooms to reduce wait time to zero.

🔋 PROJECT 04

Drive-to-Earn Reward Platform

🗓️ Jun 2021 – Aug 2021  ·  🔎 Solo Researcher  ·  👤 Individual Project
🏆 1st Place in Venture Startup Class Business Model Validation

Fintech model based on Traffic Dispersion Rewards

📊 01. Market Analysis & Opportunity

✅ Market Validation

Pedestrian reward app 'Cashwalk' reached 50B KRW revenue, proving "Action-based Reward Model". We transplant this to the high-growth EV market.

💎 Target Quality

Target 'Drivers' are high-involvement consumers with higher income than pedestrians. Premium Market with superior ad rates and conversion.

📈 EV Infra Growth

EV market predicted to grow 1,000% in 5 years, but infrastructure to consume Charging Wait Time (30~60 min) is severely lacking.

🧠 02. Core Solution: Traffic Balancing

🛑 Problem: Algorithm Blindspot

Existing navigation aims for 'Time/Distance Minimization'. Thus, detours are physically longer and considered 'Dead Routes' by algorithms.

💡 Solution: Inverse Logic

"Even if shortest route is congested, detours are inefficient (Longer Cost).
So they are never chosen without 'Incentive'."

⚙️ Logic

Offer Rewards (Cash) significant enough to offset loss, naturally dispersing traffic to non-preferred routes.
