01. Problem Discovery

Online is hype. Offline is disconnect.

Structural Issue

  • Vendor Dependency Unlike online, offline entry requires local vendors — double margins erode price competitiveness.
  • Loss of Branding Simple supply methods leave no control over VMD or customer experience.

Insight: a D2C offline hub targeting global Hallyu fandoms — where the brand controls 100% of price and experience.

02. Market Validation

Data verification — finding profitable markets via Bunjang vs Mercari.

Approach

  • Target Selection High liquidity — K-Fashion top 3 and rare K-Pop goods actively resold by JP Gen-Z. Filtered for cross-border unit economics (low volume, high margin).
  • Methodology Crawled transaction data from Bunjang (KR) and Mercari (JP) to prove a structural arbitrage price gap for identical used-good conditions.

04. Growth Strategy & Roadmap
from

Store.

to

B2B pivot.

PHASE 1 phase

Expansion

Hub Expansion

Secure traffic and logistics network via hub expansion. Aiming for omni-channel network construction, not just store opening.
Action Standardise successful Harajuku flagship operations (VMD, buying process) and expand to Osaka, Kyushu.
Logic Each hub acts as a local C2C logistics hub. Efficiency rises by linking to automated warehouses.
Goal Secure JP Gen-Z traffic and maximise seed inventory (used clothes) circulation.
PHASE 2 cash

Retention

Payment / Lock-in

Create a flywheel structure where users continuously trade within the ecosystem.
Key Feature Circulation point system: sellers receive settlement in points instead of cash.
Incentive Fee waiver and discounts when buying new items (cosmetics, clothes) with points.
Effect Prevent cash-out: maximise LTV via the sell used → point → buy new → resell cycle.
PHASE 3 b2b

Monetisation

B2B Pivot

Upgrade to a B2B solution by selling established traffic and infrastructure to KR companies.
Shift Shift profit centre from B2C (retail) to B2B (platform business).
Product A Data consulting: targeting and VMD consulting based on real-time sales and behaviour data.
Product B LaaS (Logistics-as-a-Service): lease logistics network and hubs as consignment sales channels.
05. Outcome & Impact

"Key to persuasion was not a grand vision, but a realistic strategy verified by data."

Key Evaluation Factor

  • Realistic Roadmap Highly praised for the realistic roadmap of gradual expansion starting from C2C to B2B, instead of a vain goal of building a platform at once.
  • Sharp Problem Definition Accurately struck the vendor dependency and margin structure issues of Hallyu brands and proposed practical solutions.
06. Retrospective — KPT
Keep

Data-driven approach. Verified market entry feasibility on crawled data, not intuition. Strategic detour: anticipated structural vendor backlash and built a realistic entry path via the C2C resell market.

Problem

Margin erosion. Overlooked additional cost (storage, OpEx) from customs and inspection delays — lead time delays worsened unit economics. Optimistic CAC: underestimated initial CAC by relying solely on organic viral from K-Pop fandoms.

Try

Cost buffer. Set a 10–15% buffer for logistics costs to compute conservative margin. Smoke test: measure actual CPA via small-scale performance marketing before financial planning.
